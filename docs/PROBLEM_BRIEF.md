## 1. Exact Source Baseline (`arXiv:2105.02231`)

Primary reference: Matthew Yu, *"Genus-One Data and Anomaly Detection"*, `arXiv:2105.02231` (*Phys. Rev. D* **105**, 106007, 2022). Full LaTeX source is locally available at `references/2105.02231_src/Genus1.tex` (gitignored).

### Core Construction in Yu (2021/2022)
- For a finite symmetry group $G$ of a $(1+1)$d bosonic QFT with 't Hooft anomaly $\alpha \in H^3(BG; U(1))$, Yu constructs the genus-1 moduli stack (quotient groupoid) of oriented 2-tori equipped with flat $G$-bundles:
  $$\mathcal{M}_1^G = \mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb{Z}) \backslash\!\backslash \operatorname{Hom}(\pi_1(T^2), G) /\!/ G.$$
- Integrating $\alpha$ over the universal torus bundle $\mathcal{E}_1^G \to \mathcal{M}_1^G$ produces a transgression homomorphism
  $$\int_{T^2} : H^3(BG; U(1)) \longrightarrow H^1(\mathcal{M}_1^G; U(1)).$$
  Explicitly, to an object $\rho \in \operatorname{Hom}(\pi_1(T^2), G)$ and an automorphism $(\gamma, h)$ satisfying $\gamma \triangleright \rho = \rho \triangleleft h$, this line bundle assigns the $U(1)$ Chern–Simons / Dijkgraaf–Witten phase $\langle \tilde{\rho}^* \alpha, [M_\gamma] \rangle \in U(1)$ evaluated on the closed oriented mapping torus $M_\gamma$ carrying the flat $G$-bundle $\tilde{\rho}: \pi_1(M_\gamma) \to G$ extending $\rho$ with time-direction holonomy $h$.
- **Proposition 1.1 (Yu, `Genus1.tex` L256–258, L454–584)**:
  - For every finite subgroup $G \subset \mathrm{SU}(2)$, $H^3(BG; U(1)) \cong \mathbb{Z}_{|G|}$ and $G$ has no joint categorical Schur detector (Proposition 3.2).
  - For the dicyclic group $\mathrm{Dic}_N = \langle r, s \mid r^{2N} = 1,\, s^2 = r^N,\, s r s^{-1} = r^{-1} \rangle$ of order $4N$ (in particular $Q_8 = \mathrm{Dic}_2$ of order $8$), genus-1 data $\int_{T^2}$ has an **undetectable $\mathbb{Z}_2$ kernel** generated by the unique order-2 class $2N \in H^3(B\mathrm{Dic}_N; U(1)) \cong \mathbb{Z}/(4N)\mathbb{Z}$ (for $Q_8$, element $4 \in \mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z}$).
  - Section 4 (`Genus1.tex` L585–605) illustrates this on $\widehat{\mathrm{SU}}(2)_k$ WZW with $Q_8 \subset \mathrm{SU}(2)_L$, where genus-1 modular transformations detect $k \bmod 4$ at best, missing the $\mathbb{Z}_2$ ambiguity $k \equiv 0 \text{ vs } 4 \pmod 8$.

## 2. Precise Research Questions

Let $\Sigma_g$ be a closed, oriented, **unpunctured** surface of genus $g$ with standard presentation
$$\pi_1(\Sigma_g) = \left\langle a_1, b_1, \dots, a_g, b_g \;\middle|\; \prod_{r=1}^g [a_r, b_r] = 1 \right\rangle,$$
where $[x,y] = x y x^{-1} y^{-1}$. Define the genus-$g$ stack of flat $G$-bundles
$$\mathcal{M}_g^G = \operatorname{Mod}(\Sigma_g) \backslash\!\backslash \operatorname{Hom}(\pi_1(\Sigma_g), G) /\!/ G,$$
and the transgression map $\int_{\Sigma_g} : H^3(BG; U(1)) \to H^1(\mathcal{M}_g^G; U(1))$ sending $\alpha$ to the evaluation $\langle \tilde{\rho}^* \alpha, [M_\phi] \rangle \in U(1)$ on closed oriented genus-$g$ mapping tori $M_\phi$ equipped with flat $G$-bundles $\tilde{\rho}: \pi_1(M_\phi) \to G$ (i.e., pairs $(\phi, h)$ with $\phi \in \operatorname{Aut}^+(\pi_1(\Sigma_g))$ representing $[\phi] \in \operatorname{Mod}(\Sigma_g)$ and $h \in G$ satisfying $\rho(\phi(\gamma)) = h \rho(\gamma) h^{-1}$ for all $\gamma \in \pi_1(\Sigma_g)$).

> **Important convention note**: For any closed oriented 3-manifold $M$ with classifying map $\tilde{\rho}: M \to BG$, $\langle \tilde{\rho}^* \alpha_4, [M] \rangle = (-1)^k$ where $k \in \mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z}$ is the image $\tilde{\rho}_*[M] \in H_3(BQ_8; \mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z}$ (and analogously for $\alpha_{2N} \in H^3(B\mathrm{Dic}_N; U(1)) \cong \mathbb{Z}/(4N)\mathbb{Z}$, $\langle \tilde{\rho}^* \alpha_{2N}, [M] \rangle = (-1)^k$ where $k = \tilde{\rho}_*[M] \in H_3(B\mathrm{Dic}_N; \mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}/(4N)\mathbb{Z}$). Be careful to verify orientation conventions and never sum over background bundles (summing over flat bundles produces the total DW partition function $Z(M_\phi)$, which can erase individual phases).

### Primary Target ($Q_8$, Genus 2)
1. **Existence / Obstruction at $g = 2$**: Does there exist an unpunctured genus-2 mapping torus $M_\phi$ ($\phi \in \operatorname{Mod}(\Sigma_2)$) and a flat $Q_8$-bundle $\tilde{\rho}: \pi_1(M_\phi) \to Q_8$ such that
   $$\langle \tilde{\rho}^* \alpha_4, [M_\phi] \rangle = -1$$
   (equivalently, $\tilde{\rho}_*[M_\phi]$ is **odd** in $H_3(BQ_8; \mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z}$)?
2. **Minimum Detection Genus**:
   - If **YES**: produce an explicit mapping class word $\phi$ in standard Dehn twists (Lickorish–Humphries generators), explicit flat bundle $(\rho, h)$, and independent dual-path verification of the $-1$ phase $\implies$ establishes that the minimum detection genus for $Q_8$ is **exactly $g = 2$**.
   - If **NO**: prove rigorously (covering all $\operatorname{Mod}(\Sigma_2) \times Q_8$-orbits and stabilizer generators, or via a structural topological/cohomological obstruction!) that **every** unpunctured genus-2 mapping torus with a flat $Q_8$-bundle yields phase $+1$ (i.e. $\tilde{\rho}_*[M_\phi] \in 2\mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z}$), and immediately determine the true minimum detection genus $g_{\min}(Q_8) \ge 3$ (or whether **any** unpunctured mapping torus of any genus $g$ can detect $\alpha_4$!).

### Secondary / Generalization Target ($\mathrm{Dic}_N$ and Structural Explanation)
3. **Conceptual Explanation & $\mathrm{Dic}_N$ Classification**: Explain structurally *why* the sign is $-1$ or $+1$ (e.g., via $H^*(BQ_8; \mathbb{Z}/2)$, $H_3(M_\phi; \mathbb{Z})$, Lyndon–Hochschild–Serre / Wang exact sequence of $M_\phi \to S^1$, or bordism $\Omega_3^{\mathrm{SO}}(BG) \cong H_3(BG;\mathbb{Z})$), and determine for **which dicyclic groups $\mathrm{Dic}_N$ ($N \ge 2$)** unpunctured genus-2 mapping tori detect the genus-1-invisible element $2N \in H^3(B\mathrm{Dic}_N; U(1)) \cong \mathbb{Z}/(4N)\mathbb{Z}$!
